# Overview

There are six PhD proposals below. Each is built around a problem the community still lists as open in 2026, and each is sized for a 3–4 year doctorate. Every proposal has a guaranteed core of publishable results plus a high-risk headline goal.

The problems come from three places. The first is Nowakowski's *Unsolved Problems in Combinatorial Games* ([Games of No Chance 6, 2025](https://library.slmath.org/books/Book71/files/GONC6-04.pdf)), the successor to Richard Guy's list. The second is the open-problems appendix of Siegel's *Combinatorial Game Theory* ([AMS GSM 146](https://www.ams.org/books/gsm/146/)). The third is recent arXiv and *Games of No Chance 6* papers. Problem codes such as A2 or E13 refer to Nowakowski's list.

| # | Topic | Main area | Computation | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Periodicity of octal games (.007, .6) | Impartial games | High | High |
| 2 | Invertibility and structure in restricted misère universes | Misère / algebra | Low–medium | Medium |
| 3 | Unified temperature theory, atomic weight, kos | Partizan theory | Medium | Medium |
| 4 | Complexity of game sums, poset games, Chomp | Algorithms / complexity | Medium | Medium–high |
| 5 | Scoring play, loopy games, entailing moves | Algebraic structure | Low | Medium |
| 6 | Values, temperatures and nim-dimension of placement games | Computational CGT | High | Low–medium |

This repo already contains a CGSuite 2.1.1 installer. Proposals 1, 3 and 6 can use it from the first month, and the others can use it for experiments.
